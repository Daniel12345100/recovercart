# Privacy Policy for RecoverCart

RecoverCart ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of merchants and their customers.

## Information Collection & Use
RecoverCart accesses store data (including customer abandoned checkouts and contact details) exclusively to facilitate automated WhatsApp recovery notifications on behalf of the merchant.

## Data Protection
We do not sell, rent, or share merchant or customer data with any third parties. All data is processed securely and used strictly for app functionality.

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at recovercart.official@gmail.com.
